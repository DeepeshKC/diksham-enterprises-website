ABOUT DIKSHAM

Built Around Trade.
Grounded in Expertise.

Diksham Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. is based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Its business spans Japanese selvedge denim and textile sourcing, garment production, industrial sewing machinery and commercial coordination with Japanese suppliers.

Our work brings Japanese materials and equipment into Nepal's garment industry while developing products through MITH Japanese Selvedge Denim.

Led by Diksha Dotel, Managing Director, Diksham aims to strengthen practical business relationships between Nepal and Japan.

OUR BUSINESS PORTFOLIO

What We Do.
How We Work.

From sourcing Japanese textiles and industrial equipment to developing apparel in Nepal, our activities serve both business clients and our own brand.

01 · MATERIALS

Japanese Textiles

We source Japanese selvedge denim, vintage kimono fabrics, garment textiles, hardware and related materials for product development and commercial supply.

02 · INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT

Kansai Special Machinery

Diksham has reported its appointment as the sole importer and distributor of Kansai Special industrial sewing machines in Nepal, serving garment manufacturers and industrial clients.

03 · APPAREL

Garment Development

Our primary manufacturing focus is MITH Japanese Selvedge Denim, using Japanese textile materials in garments developed and produced in Nepal.

04 · CROSS-BORDER TRADE

Business Sourcing

We coordinate with Japanese textile producers, machinery companies and suppliers to support sourcing and business-to-business trade opportunities in Nepal.

OUR BRAND

MITH Denim.
Made to Fade.

MITH Japanese Selvedge Denim brings Japanese denim fabrics together with garment development and craftsmanship in Nepal. The collection includes jeans, jackets and other denim apparel.

MITH has a retail presence in Kathmandu and dedicated websites serving Nepal and Australia. A proposed Japan-made premium line, MITH Fuji, remains under development and is not yet presented as a launched brand.
