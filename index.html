01 / About Diksham

From material sourcing to finished apparel.

Diksham Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. is based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Its activities bring together international textile sourcing, local garment production, retail, and brand development.

The company works with Japanese denim materials and related hardware for apparel produced in Nepal. Mith Japanese Selvedge Denim is the company's principal consumer-facing brand.

02 / What we do

Connected activities.
Defined roles.

01

Textile sourcing

Sourcing Japanese denim fabrics and garment-making materials, including hardware, through international commercial relationships.

02

Garment manufacturing

Developing and producing denim apparel in Nepal using sourced fabrics and local craftsmanship.

03

Brand and retail

Operating Mith Denim, including its retail presence in Lazimpat, Kathmandu, and its Nepalese online storefront.

03 / Our brand

Mith Japanese
Selvedge Denim.

A Nepalese denim brand built around Japanese fabric, local manufacturing, and considered product development.

Its range includes jeans, jackets, and other denim apparel. The brand operates a store in Lazimpat, Kathmandu, and dedicated websites serving Nepal and Australia.
