ABOUT DIKSHAM

Built Around Trade.
Grounded in Expertise.

Based in Kathmandu, Diksham Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. connects Nepalese enterprise with Japanese materials, industrial equipment and specialist sourcing networks. Our work brings together international trade and practical expertise for Nepal’s garment industry.

Alongside textile sourcing and machinery distribution, we develop and manufacture denim garments through MITH Japanese Selvedge Denim, bringing Japanese fabric traditions into products made in Nepal.

Under the leadership of Diksha Dotel, Managing Director, we focus on building dependable partnerships between Nepal and Japan.
